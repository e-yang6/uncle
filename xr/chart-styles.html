Chart styles

How the floating panels beside the mirror look. Inspired by ProjectionLab's phone app, redrawn in our own colours and type. Every chart below is drawn by the headset's own code (xr/charts.js) from one real simulation run on the sample person in CONTRACTS.md (xr/sample/charts.json).

Borrowed from the inspiration

  • One big headline number with a small spaced-caps label above it.
  • A calm curve with a soft fill under it and almost no axes: only the start and end ages, and two or three labelled points.
  • A dashed marker for what's left at the end.
  • One stacked bar for what the money is made of, with a legend of shares.
  • Milestones with progress rings and "In N years" pills, and a life drawn as a row of year ticks shaded by phase.

Left out

  • Blue, teal and purple (DESIGN-RULES: Ink, Paper, Dusk and one amber only), the night-sky scene, the cartoon avatar (we have the real face), big rounded cards.
  • Red, warnings, countdowns or a death age. The future is shown as ranges for people with your habits.
