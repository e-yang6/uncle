# Design review

1. Read DESIGN-RULES.md in full.
2. Open every screen of the live app (see DEPLOY.md for the address): the confirm screen, the summary page, and the WebXR view. Take screenshots at laptop width (1440 px) and phone width (390 px). For the headset view, use a screenshot or recording from the Quest if one is provided.
3. Compare each screenshot against every rule: colors, the one accent used once, the font, 4 px corners, no shadows, motion, tone of the words, and every item on the "Don't" list.
4. Write DESIGN-REVIEW.md as a numbered table: number, screen, problem, which rule it breaks, screenshot file, owner (from AGENTS.md), status.
5. Fix the problems in folders your human owns, one at a time, re-screenshot each fix, and mark it done. For problems in other folders, post them on the board tagged to the owner.
6. End with: how many problems found, how many fixed, and what's left.
